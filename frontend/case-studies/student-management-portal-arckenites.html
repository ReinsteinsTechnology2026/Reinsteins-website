PROJECT OVERVIEW

A Centralized System for Student Management

Reinsteins Technologies & Solutions developed a complete Student Management Portal for Arckenites, an EdTech / IT training institute, to help the organization manage student information and student groups through a centralized digital system.

The portal was built from scratch by our development team and delivered as a fully working, implemented solution — developed over approximately 2 months by a team of 3 developers and 1 tester.

THE CHALLENGE

Manual Student Database & Group Management

A Process Spread Across Separate Files

Arckenites was unable to efficiently handle its student database and student groups manually. Managing student information and student groups required maintaining details across separate files and processes rather than a single, unified system.

This consumed significant time and made it difficult to keep everything organized in one centralized place, creating the need for a purpose-built digital solution.
